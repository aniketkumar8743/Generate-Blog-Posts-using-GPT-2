Aritificial intelligence lacks the ability to draw from personal experiences and experiences of others in order to make sense of the world around it.This lack of personal experience makes it difficult for artificial intelligence to learn from its own mistakes. For example, if an AI is given a set of rules that it must follow, it will tend to follow the rules as closely as possible, even if they are not the most efficient way to achieve its goals. As a result, the AI will often make mistakes that lead it astray, leading to its eventual failure. This is why it is important for AI to be able to recognize when it has made a mistake and correct it before it causes harm to others. It is also why AI must be taught how to avoid making mistakes in the first place.
